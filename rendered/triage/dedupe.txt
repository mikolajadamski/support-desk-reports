A busy morning brings hundreds of reports about the same thing. We keep one ticket per issue
and merge every duplicate into it, so engineering sees one thread with a count.
The rule
A report is a duplicate when its subject line matches the subject of an open ticket
( decision 0001 ):
lowercase the subject and strip punctuation;
look for an open ticket with the same subject;
found one: merge the report into it. None: open a new ticket.
Matching on subject is fast and needs no reading. During an incident that matters more than
anything else. 
Try it

When you are unsure
Merge. Splitting a ticket later is cheaper than two engineers chasing the same bug.
