The three problems
parser : the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day
first, so any date with both numbers at 12 or less flips: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is what
#4471 says it is. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales .
hint : the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its
date field. People type what it asks, and the form reads the date day first. The dates flip
before they reach the parser. Only booking-v3 tenants, and only reports sent through the form.
The parser fix does not touch this. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired.
one-day : dates move by one day. These are not flips: 12 May shows as 11 May, Lisbon and
Toronto get different days for one meeting, an invite arrives before its booking. The desk
listed them in desk-notes.json and counts them under the parser by tenant; they need their own fix. They look
like a time zone bug. One reporter says they stopped reporting it, so this count is a floor.
Still open: neither the parser fix nor retiring the form explains a one-day move.
